1. Who we are

The App is operated by MEDVERİ BİLİŞİM YAZILIM TEKNOLOJİLERİ VE ELEKTRONİK HİZMETLER LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ, which is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK).

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: the username, email address and password you enter when you register; your account ID and character names.
  • Device identifier: a unique identifier of your device, sent to our servers with your account to detect cheating, multiple accounts and abuse. It is not the advertising ID and is not used for advertising.
  • Game data: your characters and progress, level, inventory, in-game currency and item movements, market and trade transactions.
  • Social data: your friends list, guild membership, in-game mail and the chat messages you send.
  • Bug and player reports: the title and description you write when you report a bug or another player, the character name involved and any in-game screenshots you choose to attach.
  • Purchase data: a record of the items you buy in the App, the transaction ID, amount and date.
  • Technical data: device model, operating system version, app version, crash and error logs, gameplay usage statistics and your IP address.
  • Notification data: if you allow notifications, your device's push token, your account ID and your in-game language.

3. What we do not collect

  • The App contains no ads. We do not read your advertising ID and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We never see or store your card number or payment details. Payments are processed by Google Play; we only verify the purchase receipt Google provides.

4. Why we use your data and our legal bases

  • To create and keep your account, run the game, save your progress, items and purchases, and provide multiplayer and chat — performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, review reports, moderate chat, keep the service secure, and measure and improve the game through usage statistics and crash reports — our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep purchase records required by tax and commercial law — legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To send push notifications and any marketing messages — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Chat and other players

Your display name, level and what you write in chat are visible to other players in the game. Chat messages are stored on our servers so we can review them when they are reported; only staff authorised for moderation can access them. You can block or report any player.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the following service providers, for the purposes stated:

  • Google — app distribution and verification of Google Play purchases.
  • OneSignal — sending push notifications. OneSignal receives your push token, account ID, in-game language, device model, operating system and app version.
  • Unity Technologies — gameplay usage statistics (Unity Analytics) and crash reports. Unity receives device and app information, in-game usage events and error logs.
  • Telegram — the messaging service we use to deliver bug and player reports to our review team. The report text, character name and any screenshots you attach reach our team this way.
  • Our server hosting and email providers.

These providers process your data only for the stated purpose and are bound to protect it. We may also disclose data to authorities where we are legally required to.

7. International transfers

We are based in Türkiye, and our servers and some of our providers are located outside the European Economic Area. When your data is transferred outside the EEA to a country without an EU adequacy decision, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another safeguard permitted by GDPR Chapter V. You can request a copy of these safeguards at destek@medveri.global.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete the data that identifies you within 30 days: your username, email address, password, device identifier, character names, friends list and push token. Your game progress and items are removed. Purchase records are not deleted: commercial and tax law require us to keep commercial records for ten years, after which they are deleted. Reported chat messages and bug or player reports are kept until the review is complete. Server logs are kept for a short period and identifying details in them are masked.

9. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and data at any time with the account deletion option in the game. If you cannot access the App, email destek@medveri.global with the subject "Kyrin2 account deletion" and your player name or ID; we will complete your request within 30 days after verifying it. Section 8 explains what is deleted and what is kept and why.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before the withdrawal. To exercise these rights, email destek@medveri.global; we will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the EU/EEA country where you live or work.

  • Notifications: turn off notifications for the App in your device settings.
  • Marketing messages: email destek@medveri.global or use the link in the message.

11. Security

We take technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss and alteration. Only staff who need the data for their work can access it. No system is perfect; if a personal data breach occurs we will notify the competent authority and, where required, you.

12. Children

The App is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. In some EU countries the age for consent to online services is higher than 13; where processing relies on consent and you are under that age, it must be given or authorised by your parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us data, email destek@medveri.global.

13. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and announce significant changes in the App. The version date is shown at the top.
